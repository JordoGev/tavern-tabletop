The Character Forge

Every hero starts somewhere. Follow these five steps to begin your journey.

  1. Choose a race.
  2. Choose a class.
  3. Roll your ability scores.
  4. Pick your equipment.
  5. Give your character a name and backstory.
Beginner's Tip: Not sure where to start? Roll a d20 for each step and let fate decide.
